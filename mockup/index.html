How we work

A clear process from first meeting to ongoing review.

You always know what happens next, who is responsible and what it costs.

  1. Discovery

    We learn about your family, business, goals and the assets you hold today.

    Weeks 1 to 2

  2. Strategy

    We present a written plan with a proposed allocation, projected outcomes and full fee disclosure.

    Weeks 3 to 4

  3. Implementation

    We transition assets in stages, managing tax and market timing along the way.

    Weeks 5 to 8

  4. Review

    Quarterly reports, an annual strategy meeting and a direct line to your adviser.

    Ongoing
